# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A single person using a desktop browser with keyboard and mouse to experiment with a track from YouTube or Spotify.

## Product Purpose

Load one track and manipulate its playback speed and pitch, either together or independently. Success means the controls are immediately understandable and useful without recreating the complexity of professional DJ software.

## Brand Personality

Functional, minimal, direct. Usefulness is delight; less is more.

## Anti-references

Dense DJ cockpit interfaces, neon gaming aesthetics, decorative greebles, and controls that do not support the core workflow.

## Design Principles

- Keep one track and one task in focus.
- Make every visible control earn its place.
- Prefer familiar browser and audio-control conventions.
- Keep provider-specific loading separate from shared playback controls.
- Reveal complexity only when the workflow requires it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Designed for desktop keyboard and mouse use with clear labels, visible states, and legible contrast. Avoid unnecessary motion and color-only meaning.
